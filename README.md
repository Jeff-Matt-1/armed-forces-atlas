# Armed Forces Atlas

So, we will build a learning app. It will be made for enthusiasts and military personnel. We will stay open source - no restriced documents. I want to build an app where one can learn about a country's armed forces: vehicles&equipment mainly. It could have a introducive learning part which explains what is tank what is warship what is APC and what is main purpose and locaiton in modern structures.  Then it would be learning blocks by blocks one subject at a time (your idea for learning principle) and after each subject it will have a comprehensive test in some way. First country we gonna try to include in app is Russian Federation with it's armed forces. Doctrinal framwork would be Grau&Bartles "The Russian Way of War". And blocks we will learn should be:
1) Ranks of ground, sea, air forces
2) Handguns
3) Heavy , squad and specialized weapons
4) Light vehicles
5) Trucks
6) Artillery and reactive artillery
7) IFV-s
8) APC-s
9) Tanks
10) EW systems
11) C2 systems
12) Radars including artillery radars
13) Engineering vehicles and equipment
14) Air defence systems
15) Reconnaisance systems (including artillery reconannisance)
16) Warships, auxillary and patrol vessels
17) Submarines
18) Drones (air, land, sea)
19) Warplanes (bomber, transport, interceptror, fighter, etc)

Main effort should be put into simplicity and focused on basic training. That is, visual recognition is the most important. Information that must be included per object is:
1) Main weaponry with it's range (if present)
2) Location within force structure (for example AK-47 - general rifle for lower echelon units or AK-12 - standard primary weapon for modern infantry squads, etc. Or BMD-4M - main IFV of air assault units. Or RPO - Chemical warfare company of infantry brigades or however it is actually named. You get the point. Student must understand where every kind of equipment is commonly found in armed forces. Could be multiple.
3) App could have a games, quizes, flashcards option to make studies fun. Or any other modern learning solution you can find effective. Let's try. Ask me if you need any details.
